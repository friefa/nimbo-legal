Help and contact

Smendy helps you quit cigarettes, vaping or cannabis: when a craving hits, you do a short breathing exercise instead of smoking.

Contact

Questions, bugs, ideas? Write to friedlfabian@gmx.de. If something isn't working, please tell us your device and system version.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Smendy has no accounts. Everything stays encrypted on your device.

How do I back up my data?
In your profile, tap “Save backup as a file”. The file is not encrypted, so please keep it somewhere safe.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
Save a backup on your old phone. On the new phone, tap “I have a backup” on first launch and pick the file, or use “Restore backup” in your profile later. Existing entries are kept; only what's missing is added.

Smendy says “I can't get to your data right now”.
This can happen after switching phones or restoring a backup. Tap “Try again”. If that doesn't help, start fresh and restore your backup.

I smoked again. Is everything gone?
No. Log it as a slip. Your XP, trophies and savings stay.

I supported Smendy. Where are my color themes?
In your profile, open “Support Smendy” and tap “Restore purchases”. Purchases belong to your Apple ID or Google account.

How do I delete everything?
In your profile, tap “Delete all data”, or uninstall the app.

Reminders don't show up.
Check in your system settings that notifications are allowed for Smendy, and in your profile that reminders are on.

Health notice

Smendy does not replace advice from a doctor or therapist. Only do the “Power Breath” exercise while sitting or lying down. Never while driving, in water or standing up. If you feel dizzy, breathe normally. Free support for quitting: in the US, call 1-800-QUIT-NOW; in the UK, the NHS Quit Smoking service.
