The short version
  • Smendy works without an account. You don't need to sign up.
  • Everything you enter in Smendy is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database.
  • We never receive this data. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • While you use it, the app does not connect to any server of ours (fonts and graphics are bundled with the app). The only exception is the optional “Support Smendy” purchases: prices, purchasing and restoring go through the App Store or Google Play (section 7).
  • Your health data is never sent to Apple, Google or us in the process.

Information about your use of tobacco, nicotine and cannabis is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. It is processed only locally on your device, because you enter it yourself to use the app's features. It is never transferred to us or to third parties.

Encryption: The database is encrypted with SQLCipher. The key is generated randomly on first launch and stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.

3. Permissions

Notifications (optional): only if you agree. Smendy schedules reminders locally on your device; no push servers are used. You can turn reminders off at any time in your profile or in your system settings.

Vibration (haptics): to pace the breathing exercises. No data is collected.

Smendy does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

4. Backup and switching phones

In your profile you can export all your entries as a file (JSON). The file is handed to the share sheet of your device, and you choose where it goes (e.g. the Files app or email). What happens to the file after that is up to you and the service you choose. The file is not encrypted and contains your health data, so please keep it somewhere safe.

You can restore such a file (“Restore backup” in your profile, or “I have a backup” on first launch). It is read only on your device and added to the encrypted database. On iPhone, the encrypted database may also be part of your iCloud or device backup with Apple; on Android, Smendy is excluded from system backups.

5. Deleting your data

In your profile, “Delete all data” removes all entries and settings from your device after asking you to confirm; scheduled reminders are cancelled as well. All data is also deleted when you uninstall the app. Purchased styles stay linked to your store account and can be brought back with “Restore purchases”.

6. App stores

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data according to their own privacy policies; we have no influence on this. If you allow your operating system to share crash reports or usage statistics, we only receive them in anonymized, aggregated form through Apple's or Google's developer consoles.

7. Purchases (“Support Smendy”)

You can support Smendy with optional one-time purchases, which unlock extra color themes. Purchasing, payment and receipts are handled entirely by Apple or Google and your account with them (Apple ID, Google account); their terms and privacy policies apply. For this, the app asks the store for prices, starts the purchase and checks which purchases belong to your store account (“Restore purchases”). The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We receive no payment details, no name and no email address, only the aggregated sales reports in the developer consoles. On your device, the app only stores which styles are unlocked. There is no server of ours, no payment provider and no link to your health data.

9. Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we only use your email address and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). We cannot provide information about your app data or delete it for you, because we never receive it. It is entirely in your hands (export, delete, uninstall).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany.

11. Health notice

Smendy supports you in quitting, but it does not replace advice from a doctor or therapist. Only do the “Power Breath” exercise while sitting or lying down. Never while driving, in water or standing up.
